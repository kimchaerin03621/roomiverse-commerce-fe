## comp-1.png: 기본안: 크레인 운전석 높이에서 컨테이너 협곡을 내려다보는 장면

```
Design a desktop website intro screen, 16:9 landscape (1920x1080), rendered as the web page itself edge to edge (no browser window, no device frame, no mockup, no letterbox bars).

Brand: ROOMi (ROOMiVERSE), a DJ lifestyle brand: any ordinary space can become your own party, anywhere. A shipping container is the brand's metaphor: it goes anywhere and can hold anything. Use the attached image as the logo (a hand-drawn marker graffiti wordmark "ROOMi"), rendered in white.

This screen contains ONLY three things, nothing else (no headline, no copy, no buttons, no badges, no catalog numbers):
1. A thin, quiet top navigation bar over the image: the ROOMi logo at the exact horizontal center; left: "MEET ROOMi", "WHAT'S GOING ON", "LET'S PARTY", "SHOP"; right: "CART (0)", "MY". Small white uppercase grotesk sans, generous spacing, no background bar.
2. A full-bleed photoreal cinematic scene of a real container port, like a still from a big-budget film: high camera angle looking DOWN onto the containers, anamorphic lens character, subtle film grain, atmospheric haze. Color grade at dusk in the brand palette: hot pink #F71D64 and violet #C14ED4 in the sky and its reflections, soft peach #FFB792 haze near the horizon, deep black #111111 shadows. The scene must read as clearly separated depth layers (foreground, middle, background) because it will become a 2.5D parallax.
3. The trigger: exactly ONE container in the scene is the way in. Its doors are slightly open and a warm hot-pink light glows from inside; that light alone is what draws the eye. No marker, icon, label or arrow anywhere in the scene.

Composition: viewpoint from high up a gantry crane, looking down at about 40 degrees into a long canyon of stacked containers on a wet, reflective concrete apron. Foreground: a dark steel crane beam crossing the top-left corner, out of focus. Middle: the container stacks in muted reds, blues and grays, with the single glowing container sitting at the lower-center focal point. Background: quay cranes, a docked ship, the sea and a pink-violet dusk sky with peach haze on the horizon.
One dominant move: the single glowing container. Everything else is calm, cinematic and still.
```
