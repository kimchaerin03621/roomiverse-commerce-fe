# 3D Shop 시안 프롬프트 (3장, 컨테이너 내부 · 소박한 3D 렌더)

사용법: 시안마다 ChatGPT 새 대화를 열고 로고(`public/logo.png`)를 첨부한 뒤 붙여넣으세요. kidsuper 스크린샷이 있으면 "render style reference"라고 적어서 같이 첨부하면 더 좋아요. 결과는 `.impeccable/mocks/shop/`에 파일명 그대로 저장합니다.

## bedroom.png: Container bedroom

```
Design a desktop web page screen, 16:9 landscape (1920x1080), rendered as the web page itself edge to edge (no browser window, no device frame, no mockup).

Brand: ROOMi (ROOMiVERSE), a DJ lifestyle brand: any ordinary space can become your own party, anywhere. The visitor has just stepped INTO a shipping container at a harbor. This screen is ROOMi's 3D Shop: the INSIDE of that 20ft shipping container, converted into a small room, seen from one fixed camera.

RENDER STYLE (very important): a deliberately simple, slightly naive real-time 3D render, like an indie game or a Blender toy scene: low-poly models with chunky bevels, hand-painted textures, soft baked lighting and ambient occlusion, a little wobbly and imperfect on purpose. NOT photoreal, NOT a photograph. Think of a charming handmade 3D diorama.

The container is clearly readable: corrugated steel walls and ceiling painted on the inside, a plywood floor, and the solid corrugated END WALL of the container facing the camera. The container doors are behind the camera and closed: NO opening, NO window, NO view of the outside anywhere. It is a closed, cozy box lit only from inside by warm practical lamps with soft brand-colored accents: hot pink #F71D64, violet #C14ED4, peach #FFB792, deep black #111111.

The page contains ONLY:
1. A thin top navigation bar over the image: the attached logo (a white two-wordmark graffiti "ROOMi ROOMi") at the exact horizontal center; left "MEET ROOMi", "WHAT'S GOING ON", "LET'S PARTY", "SHOP"; right "CART (0)", "MY". Small white uppercase grotesk, no background bar.
2. The full-bleed container-room scene.
3. Bottom-right corner, small white uppercase text: "VIEW ALL →". Nothing else: no headline, no prices, no cards, no badges.

Objects that MUST be in the container, each clearly separate with some space around it (each will be a clickable 3D object):
- a sleek two-deck DJ controller with no brand logo, the hero product, near the center
- a folded slogan towel (no readable text), a baseball cap, a small mini-CD keyring, a folded bandana, and an embroidered English-letter patch, placed casually on shelves, a chair, a hook or a table
- a lamp or LED light bar (it will switch the light mood)
- a turntable or a pair of speakers (it will play music)

Room inside the container: a cozy bedroom. A low mattress with a blanket along the left wall, a small desk against the right wall holding the DJ controller, a clamp lamp, a wall shelf and hooks with the goods, a small speaker pair on the floor. Camera at standing eye level just inside, looking down the length of the container toward its closed end wall.

One dominant move: the converted container interior at rest, with the DJ controller as the quiet focal point.
```
